Recoil Clash: Gun Duel — Privacy Policy

App: Recoil Clash: Gun Duel (package com.cozygames.recoilclash)
Version covered: 0.8 and forward, until this policy is amended.
Effective date: 2026-09-02.
Last updated: 2026-09-02.

Recoil Clash is a physics duel game published by Cozygames. It has no account and no login. The small amount of data it handles is for showing ads, understanding which levels players reach, and fixing crashes. Nothing collected identifies you personally.

What we collect

  • Advertising ID. Google's resettable advertising identifier, used by the ad SDKs to show ads and limit how often you see the same one. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Gameplay events. Aggregate events like "level completed", which weapon was used, and session counts, sent to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which features players use. Bound to an anonymous identifier, never to your name or email.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a technical report (stack trace, device model, Android version, app version) so we can fix the bug. No personal content is included.
  • Ad performance data. When an ad is shown, we record its estimated value and which ad network served it, in aggregate, to run the business. This uses the same anonymous identifiers as above.
  • Approximate, non-precise signals used by ads. The ad SDKs may use coarse, IP-derived region and device information (device model, Android version, language) to serve and measure ads. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Build identifier. The version of the app you're running, used to filter analytics and crashes by release.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your precise location (no GPS).
  • Your contacts, photos, videos, or files.
  • Your microphone, camera, or audio.
  • Your payment details. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or billing information, only an anonymous confirmation that the purchase succeeded.
  • Any browsing data outside the app.

Ads

Recoil Clash is free and supported by ads through Google AdMob. It shows interstitial ads (between some levels) and rewarded ads (which you choose to watch in exchange for a revive or a weapon trial). The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase permanently removes interstitials; rewarded ads remain available because they are always your choice.

AdMob acts as a mediator and may hand an ad request to a partner ad network. The app includes the SDKs of these networks, and when one of them serves an ad it receives the same ad signals described above:

  • Pangle — Pangle privacy policy
  • ironSource (Unity) — Unity privacy policy
  • Consent (EEA, UK, and similar regions). On first launch we show Google's consent form (UMP). Your choice decides whether you see personalized or non-personalized ads, and you can revisit it any time via the Privacy button on the main menu.
  • You can reset or limit ad tracking any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization).
  • See the Google Ads privacy & terms and AdMob documentation for details on how Google uses this data.

Where the data goes

Data leaves your device only to reach these services, over an encrypted (TLS) connection:

  • Google AdMob (and, through its mediation, Pangle and ironSource) — serves and measures ads. AdMob policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — receives the aggregate gameplay events. Firebase privacy.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — receives crash reports.
  • Google Firebase Remote Config — downloads game settings (for example, ad frequency) to your device. This is a download, not a collection of your data.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional Remove Ads purchase. Google privacy policy.

Your level progress, weapon unlocks, and settings stay on your device (local storage).

Children

Recoil Clash features stylized cartoon gun duels and is intended for a general audience aged 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us at the address below and we'll delete any associated records.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall removes all local save data from your device.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads to break the link the ad SDKs use.
  • To request deletion of any analytics or crash records, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or where it goes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page changes too. Significant changes will be flagged inside the app on next launch.
